## 🎮 Childhood & Internet Arrival (2002 – 2013)

### Reaching the Internet Early & Playing Online Games
In 2002, when the Internet was first introduced across Vietnam, I stepped into local gaming cafes in my hometown of Tam Ky. My childhood revolved around games like *MU Online*, *Yahoo Messenger*, *Võ Lâm Truyền Kỳ*, *Space Cowboy*, and *Perfect World*.

It was a childhood of walking barefoot, escaping home, and climbing fences to sit in net cafes—often facing strict discipline and beatings with switches from my parents. In real life, I was just an ordinary, anonymous kid. But inside the game, I was a dedicated hero grinding through levels, hunting monsters, and collecting loot. That childhood—though filled with hardship—remains a beautiful memory that I will cherish for the rest of my life.

## 💻 Falling in Love with C# & Building Software (2016 – 2018)

### Year 2 of University & The Shoe Store Management App
My second year of university was my most successful academic period. I fell in love with **C#**. I decided to build a complete desktop application to manage a shoe store (as I loved sneakers).

- Finding keywords in Vietnamese was tough back then. I translated VB.net code snippets into C# using online converter tools.
- I grasped recursion in practice: handling auto-incrementing ID indices using try-catch blocks to increment indices whenever product array lengths mismatched due to deleted items.
- I designed custom color ID schemas using Hashtables (`00` for white, `11` for black, `01` for white-black, `22` for red, `12` for red-black).
- Inspired by barcode scanners at my local Tam Ky supermarket, I researched and integrated **Code128** barcode generation into the software.
- After 3 intense months of work, I completed a market-ready software application.

### Reaching Top 3 in Class
For the first time in my life, I felt truly capable. I achieved top grades in SQL and C#, ranking in the **Top 3 of my class**. It proved that when you work with genuine passion, you achieve extraordinary results.

### Exploring Cross-Platform Tech: WinForms, WPF, Xamarin & Firebase
I wanted to expand beyond WinForms. I researched WPF, MVVM, Entity Framework, LINQ, and asked: *"Why isn't there something you write once that runs on every platform?"*

That led me to **Xamarin**, Google Sheets API streaming, Azure, and **Firebase**. To achieve real-time streaming data, I explored Ionic, Android, Cordova, and ultimately **Progressive Web Apps (PWA)**.

## 🧠 Engineering Kitwork Engine & Agent Runtime (2024 – 2026)

Building upon a decade of self-hosting and platform development, I spent 2024–2026 designing **Kitwork Engine**:
- **Custom Stack-based Bytecode VM in Pure Go**: Zero CGO, ~14MB binary size, near-instant startup.
- **24-Byte Value System**: Memory-optimized NaN-boxed tagged union struct.
- **JS Subset & Energy Gas Accounting**: Arrow-only functions, no `while`, no `try-catch`, with opcode energy gas limits (`MaxEnergy`).
- **Production Dogfooding**: Shipped **KitURL** and built **Kitwork Agent Runtime** (durable multi-tenant AI Agent execution with SQLite state persistence, Skill contracts, and **Human Approval Checkpoints** powering WithAI Newsroom).
