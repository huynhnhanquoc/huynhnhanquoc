## 🌊 Where It All Began — Da Nang

In **2019**, in **Da Nang** — often called *Vietnam’s most livable city* — my journey into technology began.

I was in my early twenties, burning with passion and the wild belief that I could build a platform that would change the world.

Those were the days when I worked until time disappeared —
leaving home before dawn, arriving at the office as the first sunlight hit the windows, and returning only when the city lights had long awakened.

What was I chasing?
Perhaps a dream too fragile to explain — a dream only a kindred soul could understand.

In those lonely nights, I gave myself a name: **Song** — like a reflection in a mirror. Solitary, but steadfast.

I dreamed of building a **startup** that could write code powerful enough to shift the world.
But then, the dream collapsed. The startup failed.
And I was left with nothing but my two hands and the lingering echo of that fire.

I went back to my hometown — **Tam Ky** — carrying failure, but also a small, unextinguished flame.
That’s when I realized:

> 

The dream was never gone.
It was just waiting for me to be strong enough to continue it.

## 🌙 Living Two Lives

Back then, I lived as two different people.

By day, I worked as a **delivery driver**, surviving one order at a time.
By night, I sat in my small room, fingers typing lines of code as if they were lifelines holding me together.

I built **websites** to keep my servers alive.
Tried **affiliate marketing** just to make ends meet.
There were nights when my body felt broken, but the fire in my heart wouldn’t let me stop.

Because I believed:
If I could keep the passion alive — if I could endure —
one day my dream of a **Golang-based independent platform** would come true.

## 🧭 Learning Again — From the Roots

There was a time I believed **Angular** or **RxJS** could solve everything.
But I soon realized — depending too much on frameworks only distances you from the essence of programming.

When I faced **SEO challenges**, I discovered the quiet strength of **static web pages**.

So I returned to **Golang** — a language that felt solid, free, and close to who I was.
At the same time, I forced myself to **relearn JavaScript from the ground up**.

No skipping fundamentals.
No hiding behind frameworks.
Just me and the **DOM**, learning to manipulate it, writing small libraries to solve my own problems.

That’s when I understood:

> 

Frameworks are tools.
Real power lies in how you think.

## ⚙️ Dynamic Stack Web — From Idea to Reality

I started with **static websites**, tuned for SEO — but I didn’t want to stop there.
I wanted them to **live**, to breathe, to become real applications.

The concept of **hydration** in **Jamstack** inspired me —
a server delivering static HTML that **JavaScript wakes up** into a dynamic experience.

I built my own interpretation and called it **Dynamic Stack Web** —
a system combining the reliability of the **server** and the flexibility of the **client**.

In this model, **JavaScript** is no longer the whole story —
just one piece of a much bigger picture.

## 💡 Lessons From the Journey

From **Da Nang** to **Tam Ky**,
from bitter failure to countless nights in front of my laptop,
I’ve learned three timeless truths:

- 

**Perseverance** is the flame that never dies.

- 

**Failure** is a harsh but honest teacher.

- 

**Problem-solving mindset** matters more than any technology.

Technology changes every day.
But patience and clarity — they take you the farthest.

## 🌱 Spreading the Dream

Today, my dream is no longer a private one.
I want to **share it** — the spirit of open coding, open learning, and open creation.

To tell my story not to impress, but to remind others:
If you’re quietly building something, nurturing your dream alone,
you are not alone.

I no longer dream of redefining the world.
I dream of **redefining myself** —
and if, along the way, I can touch a few like-minded souls,
that’s already happiness enough.

## ☀️ Memories That Stay

**Da Nang** — the city where my tech dream was once forgotten —
is now a memory I can’t separate from who I am.

It reminds me:
No matter how many times you fall,
as long as the fire in your heart still burns,
your dream will never disappear.

Every day I sit down and type the first few lines of code,
I know I’m alive —
truly alive — in a simple, quiet kind of happiness.

I still remember the afternoons in Da Nang,
sunlight pouring through the trees along **Tran Phu Street**.

Back then, I dreamed of success.
But today, I understand something deeper:

> 

Growth is the truest success.

For me, success is no longer a spotlight.
It’s peace.
It’s sharing.
It’s nurturing a dream —
and doing my small part to make this world a little better.
